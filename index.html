About

Notes

I'm an aerosapce engineering student, studying at Virginia Tech. Working through my junior year I look to graduate in May of 2028. I pride myself in fact that I'm always up for a challenge. I find I learn best from trying new things & tackling tough problems.

The wind tunnel is my most ambitious project yet. The complete rig is set to be over a meter long with every aspect of the model created by me. I 3D model everything in SOLIDWORKS, slice it & get it ready for the 3D printer, & finally print the part myself. Because I care about aesthetics, I've made the commitment to sand, fill, & paint all my parts to look like a professional, finished project. This is still a work in progress, but I encourage you to click the sheet above to follow along with me on this journey!

I'm currently looking for a Summer 2027 engineer internship related to my major. This past summer I've gotten so much experience with my engineering internship at PayneCrest Electric, an electrical contractor. I was on-site at Boeing designing & detailing the electrical work for an aircraft hanger expansion.
